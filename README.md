# Early-Warning OS

AI Migration & Population Early-Warning Intelligence Pipeline, also known as Immigration Alert.

## Purpose

Early-Warning OS collects and analyses signals on migration and population movements, then turns them into timely, actionable alerts.

## Approach

The system follows the CXL model: a personal OS for knowledge and daily work, a marketing brain for audience and messaging, and a campaign engine for turning alerts into outreach.

## Status

Early setup. Structure and components are being defined.
