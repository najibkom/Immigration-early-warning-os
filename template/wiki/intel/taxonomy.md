# Signal taxonomy

Starter codes. Confirm with the client in Phase 2; add codes, never reuse one for another meaning.

## SOC — Social signal (aggregate only)
- SOC-VOL — volume velocity on displacement/transit topics
- SOC-SENT — sentiment shift
- SOC-SRCH — search frequency (routes, visas, jobs abroad)
- SOC-NOISE — viral chatter unrelated to displacement (used to dampen)

## ECO — Economic stress
- ECO-FOOD — staple food prices
- ECO-FX — currency depreciation
- ECO-JOB — unemployment, youth unemployment
- ECO-REM — remittance flows
- ECO-SHOCK — sudden shock (plant closure, harvest loss, trade halt)

## ENV — Environmental impact
- ENV-DRT — drought index / rainfall deficit
- ENV-FLD — flood
- ENV-HEAT — extreme heat
- ENV-CROP — crop failure / livestock loss

## POL — Policy shift
- POL-VISA — visa rule change
- POL-BRD — border opening/closure, controls
- POL-ASY — asylum procedure change
- POL-LAB — labour migration programme change
- POL-CONF — contradictory official texts (forces human hold)

## CTX — Context (not scored)
- CTX-CONF — conflict or security event
- CTX-HUM — humanitarian response already under way
